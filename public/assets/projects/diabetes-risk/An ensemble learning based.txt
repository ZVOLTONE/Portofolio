An ensemble learning-based diabetes risk detection app. Developed in response to the difficulties faced by users in assessing their own risk of diabetes. This app assesses risk based on the user’s demographic details and daily activities. The model was built from a dataset of 100,000 records using a combination of Random Forest (Bagging Ensemble) and LightGBM (Gradient Boosting), with META-LEARNER (Stacking Ensemble) employed to produce an accurate model.
