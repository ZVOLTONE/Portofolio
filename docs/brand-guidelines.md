## 1. Brand Identity & Persona

### Core Positioning
**Yudhistira Ari Nugroho** is a high-achieving Computer Science student at BINUS University specializing in **Backend Development, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence**. With a proven record in competitive programming (ICPC Asia Jakarta 2025 Honorable Mention), software laboratory teaching, and student leadership, his brand communicates technical rigor, reliability, and continuous innovation.

### Brand Pillars
1. **Engineering Rigor & Architecture:** Clean code, robust backend design, scalability, and algorithmic thinking.
2. **AI & Emerging Tech:** Practical implementation of AI agents, machine learning, and data intelligence.
3. **Leadership & Mentorship:** Academic mentoring at BINUS Software Laboratory, team leadership, and cross-functional stakeholder management.
4. **Impact & Problem Solving:** Award-winning design thinking and practical software problem solving.
